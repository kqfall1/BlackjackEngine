Even before I became a programmer, I always have wanted to program a blackjack game of my own - for no particular reason. 
I've never played it in person and am far from being an expert in the game, but have always been drawn to its subtle 
complexity and have attempted more than once to create a solution of my own for the game. 

This latest attempt will be my last - I have finally arrived at an elegant solution and am proud of the resulting 
codebase. I would like to highlight my TestDeck abstraction that allows me to use pre-determined card sequences in 
testing scenarios for convenient QA and my BlackjackRulesetConfiguration class and BlackjackRuleset interface that allow 
clients to customize game experiences (I also have provided a convenience class StandardBlackjackRuleset for normal, 
North American blackjack rules). 

This will be a long-standing, ongoing project of mine; feel free to use my work. I plan on building multiple different
apps on top of this engine throughout the years.
